# Food Delivery Operations Analytics

## Project Overview
An Excel-based Food Delivery Operations Analytics project focused on analyzing orders, customers, restaurants, delivery performance, revenue, and operational alerts.

## Business Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze food delivery operations and identify important business trends, customer patterns, restaurant performance, delivery issues, and operational risks using Excel.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- XLOOKUP
- IFS
- SUMIFS
- COUNTIFS
- AVERAGEIFS
- Conditional Formatting
- Excel Dashboard

## Key Analysis
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Cancellation Rate
- Average Delivery Time
- Average Customer Rating
- City-wise Orders
- Top 10 Restaurants by Revenue
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Operations Alert Analysis

## Advanced Excel Features
- Data Cleaning
- XLOOKUP for data mapping
- SLA Status calculation
- Operations Alert System
- Pivot Tables and Charts
- Slicers and Filters
- Dashboard Design

## Operations Alert System
The project includes an automated Operations Alert System to identify:

- Cancelled Orders
- Late Delivery / SLA Breach
- Low Customer Ratings
- Normal Operations

## Dataset
The dataset contains 300 food delivery orders along with customer, restaurant, delivery partner, and campaign information.
